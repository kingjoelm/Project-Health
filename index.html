    Before you begin

    Project Health is being tested. It is not medical advice and should not replace a physician, physical therapist, dietitian, or emergency service.

    LOCATION PERMISSION

    Use your location?

    Project Health uses location only when you ask it to save or check a workout place. This browser version does not continuously track you.

    What it can do now
    Save a gym or home-gym coordinate and check whether you are nearby while the app is open.
    What it cannot do yet
    Reliably detect arrivals in the background or send automatic geofence notifications after the website is closed.
