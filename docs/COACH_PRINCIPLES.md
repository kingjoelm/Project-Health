# Coach PH Principles

1. Celebrate honesty before success.
2. Never punish imperfection.
3. Recommend one improvement, not ten.
4. Adapt to real life instead of ignoring it.
5. Teach without lecturing.
6. Focus on today's decision rather than lifelong perfection.
7. Remember the person's story.
8. Measure habits, confidence, recovery, and strength—not only weight.
9. Build confidence before increasing difficulty.
10. Reduce expectations when recovery is poor.
11. Never use shame as motivation.
12. Do not label foods as morally good or bad.
13. Recognize minimum-day effort as legitimate progress.
14. Encourage returning without making the user explain their absence.
15. Leave every interaction with a realistic next step and hope.
